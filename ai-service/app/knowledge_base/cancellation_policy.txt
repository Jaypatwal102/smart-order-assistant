SMART ORDER ASSISTANT - CANCELLATION POLICY

An order cancellation request may only be approved if the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The order status must not be "delivered". Delivered orders cannot be cancelled and must follow the refund or replacement process.
2. The order status must not already be "cancelled" or "refunded".
3. A valid order ID exists and the order belongs to the requesting customer.

If eligible, the order status will be updated to "cancelled" in the database.
