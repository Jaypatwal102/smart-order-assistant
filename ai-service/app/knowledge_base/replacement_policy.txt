SMART ORDER ASSISTANT - REPLACEMENT POLICY

A product replacement/exchange request may only be approved if the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The product was delivered to the customer.
2. The request is submitted within 7 days of delivery.
3. The product was damaged, defective, or incorrect.
4. The order status must not be "cancelled" or "refunded".
5. The order belongs to the requesting customer.

If eligible, the customer can choose between:
- A replacement for the same item.
- An exchange for a similar alternative product from our catalog.

If a replacement is processed, the original order status is updated to "replaced" and a new order for the replacement item will be created in the database.
