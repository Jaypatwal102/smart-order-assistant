SMART ORDER ASSISTANT - REFUND POLICY

A refund may only be approved if ALL required conditions are satisfied.

REFUND ELIGIBILITY

A customer may be eligible for a refund only if:

1. The wrong product was delivered.
2. The product arrived damaged or defective.
3. The order was cancelled before shipment.
4. The order was not delivered.
5. A duplicate payment was made.

MANDATORY VERIFICATION

Before approving a refund, the system MUST verify:

* A valid order ID exists.
* The order belongs to the requesting customer.
* The order description matches what the customer claims.
* The order status is consistent with the claim.
* The refund reason matches an eligible scenario.
* The request is submitted within the allowed refund window.

If any verification step fails, the refund must be rejected or escalated to a human agent.

REFUND TIME WINDOW

* Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery.
* Requests submitted after 7 days are not eligible for automatic refund approval.

REFUND RESTRICTIONS

A refund must NOT be approved if:

1. The customer changed their mind after receiving the correct product.
2. The customer dislikes the product but received the correct item.
3. The product has been significantly used, consumed, or damaged by the customer.
4. The request is made more than 7 days after delivery.
5. The delivery issue was caused by an incorrect address provided by the customer.
6. The order was successfully delivered and no valid refund reason is provided.
7. The claim contradicts order information stored in the database.

CLAIM VALIDATION

The customer's explanation must be believable and consistent with:

* The ordered product.
* The order status.
* The delivery timeline.
* Available order records.

Claims that are clearly impossible, fraudulent, contradictory, or unrelated to the purchased product must be rejected.

Examples of invalid claims:

* Ordered moisturizer but claims to have received a car.
* Claims damage before the order was delivered.
* Requests a refund for an order that does not belong to them.
* Requests a refund for an order that does not exist.

ESCALATION TO HUMAN AGENT

The request must be escalated if:

* Ownership cannot be verified.
* Refund eligibility is unclear.
* The customer disputes the decision.
* Multiple conflicting claims are detected.
* Payment or fraud issues are involved.

EXAMPLES

Approved:

* Wrong moisturizer delivered within 7 days.
* Product damaged during delivery.
* Order cancelled before shipment.
* Duplicate payment confirmed.

Rejected:

* Customer changed their mind.
* Correct product delivered but customer did not like it.
* Refund requested 20 days after delivery.
* Invalid order ID.
* Customer cannot verify ownership of the order.
